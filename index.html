A new chapter. A new BEGINNING. A new SOUND.

For many of us, the UNITED story began with young people gathering - powerful prayer meetings, loud and passionate praise, intimate moments of worship - hands raised and hearts abandoned.

A generation hungry and desperate for the tangible presence of God, lifting up the name of Jesus with one heart, one voice and one sound.

THE SOUND OF FREEDOM.
THE SOUND OF CHAINS FALLING.
THE SOUND OF REVIVAL.

In 2027, a new and independent chapter begins.

UNITED Nights is a new gathering of worshippers, songwriters, musicians and creatives seeking God and making space for encounters with His presence.

We are not trying to recreate yesterday. We honour what God has done in the past, but we are looking forward to the future - a clean slate, new wine and a fresh outpouring of the Holy Spirit.

We are simply making room for whatever God wants to do next.
